1Getting started

What it is

One platform for the whole business: products, stock, purchases, sales, credit, payroll, withdrawals, cash and results.

Where to find it

  • Log in with your email and password. Accounts are created by the Super Admin. On your first login you choose a new password.
  • Choose a service on Choose a service: Hair or Fashion (Mode). Housing and Transport are coming soon. Use Switch service at the top to change.
  • The side menu shows only the modules your role allows. On a phone, open it with the menu button.
  • Change the language (English / French) at the top of the screen.

Good to know

  • Built for phones first: long lists turn into cards and buttons are easy to tap.
  • Locations (shops and warehouses) are shared by every service. Each service keeps its own stock, sales and figures at a shop.
  • All amounts are in US dollars (USD). Days are counted in Lubumbashi time.
  • The New sale button floats at the bottom right for people who can sell.

2Home

What it is

Your daily briefing for the service: a greeting, a quote of the day, what needs your attention, and your modules with one figure each.

How it works

  • Needs attention lists only what you can act on, most urgent first: Out of stock, Low stock, Review pending approvals, Record today's sales (from 16:00 if nothing was sold today), Customers owe money, days without sales this month, stock to receive, products to confirm, requisitions ready to buy.
  • Each row disappears once it's dealt with. When nothing is left you see All caught up.
  • Module cards show one figure, e.g. sales today, units on hand, approvals waiting.

Who can use it

Everyone. Each person sees only the figures their role allows.

4Products and prices

What it is

The catalogue of what each service sells, and the selling prices.

Where to find it

Products → Catalogue and Prices. Lists of lengths, sizes and colours are in Product settings at the bottom of the side menu.

How it works

  • Each product gets an automatic code (SKU), e.g. HAIR-00001 or FASH-00001.
  • Hair products are told apart by length, colour and texture. Fashion products by size and colour, with one photo (up to 5 MB).
  • A product made by someone who can't confirm products waits as pending until a chief confirms it.
  • Prices shows each product's selling price next to its purchase cost, stock and last sale. The selling price fills the sale form automatically.
  • A low stock level can be set per product. Below it, the product shows as Low stock.

Who can use it

Most roles create products. Chiefs confirm them. Chief Admin and Chief Sales Admin set prices. Chief Admin and Chief Inventory Admin manage Product settings.

Good to know

A product already used in stock or a requisition is archived, never deleted. Deleting an unused product needs an approval.

5Requisitions

What it is

A shop's request for stock to buy.

How it works

  1. Requisitions → New requisition: choose the location, add products and quantities. Or leave it empty and describe the need in the note. The buyer then chooses the products.
  2. Submit for approval. The Chief Admin approves or rejects it in Approvals. A rejected one can be revised and sent again.
  3. Approved requisitions appear under Ready to purchase. Then they become Purchasing, then Closed. Products the buyer adds are marked Added by buyer.

Who can use it

Chief Sales Admin creates. Chief Admin approves.

6Stock

What it is

Everything about stock: buying abroad, receiving in Goma, sending to shops and people, and seeing what is where.

Where to find it

Stock → Overview, Purchase batches, Distributions.

How it works

  • Purchase batches (one buying trip): the buyer adds approved requisition lines and extra products, with the quantity and unit cost of each, or why it wasn't bought. Trip expenses (transport, freight, customs…) are added with a receipt, at any time. Then Mark as purchased → approval → Mark shipped → Mark arrived.
  • Receiving in Goma: for each line, count the good and damaged pieces. Missing pieces are calculated for you. Confirm receipt. Only good pieces become sellable stock, as a lot at its purchase cost.
  • Distributions: send stock from the business to a shop or a person. The Chief Admin approves it, and the stock moves only then.
  • Overview: units on hand, stock value, and three views: By product (status In stock / Low stock / Out of stock / Never stocked, with history and CSV), By lot and By holder.

Who can use it

Chief Inventory Admin buys. Manager Admin and Chief Sales Admin receive and distribute. Chief Admin approves. Stock is visible to most roles, but not to Sales Agents.

Good to know

Trip expenses never change product costs. Stock can never go below zero. A distribution approved after the stock ran out is refused and nothing moves.

7Sales

What it is

Recording sales on the phone in the shop, and the list of every sale.

How it works

  1. Sales → New sale. Pick the day: today, or up to 7 days back for a sale recorded late. The Super Admin can pick any past day from 1 January 2026.
  2. Search a product, choose the lot, the quantity and the price. The selling price is filled in. A different price needs a short reason.
  3. The agreed total can be lowered for a whole-sale discount, with a reason.
  4. Payment: Cash, Mobile money or Credit (see Credit). Check the sale, then record it.

Fixing a sale

  • Request correction on a sale: change quantities, prices, the total, cash ↔ mobile money, the customer name or the date (up to 7 days back, any past day for the Super Admin), with a reason. Nothing changes until the Super Admin approves.
  • Exchange products (cash and mobile money sales, up to 30 days): the customer brings products back and takes others. If the customer pays the difference, it's recorded at once. Money given back needs approval.

Finding the day of a sale

  • Sales → Check sales: Add a product for each product you remember (the exact length, size and colour), a quantity if you know it, and the dates (the last 30 days by default). Then tap Check.
  • You see the days, per shop, that sold all these products. The day with the fewest other products comes first. Quantities never hide a day: differences are just shown.
  • If no day sold them all, you see the closest days, for example "4 of 5 products", and which products are missing.
  • Tap a day to see its sales next to your list. To move a sale to the right day, open it and use Request correction.
  • Sales Agents only search their own shop. Nothing is changed by checking.

Who can use it

Sales Agents sell in their own shop only. Chief Sales Admin and Super Admin sell anywhere.

Good to know

You can't sell more than the shop holds: if two people sell the last piece at once, only one sale goes through. Margins always use the purchase cost at the time of the sale.

8Credit

What it is

Sales paid later: who owes what, and every payment received.

Where to find it

Sales → Credit: total owed, customers who still owe, their open sales and payments.

How it works

  • In a credit sale, pick the customer or add a New customer, and enter what was Paid now (0 or more).
  • Record a payment later. It can be dated up to 7 days back (any past day for the Super Admin). A payment goes to the oldest open sale first, and never more than what is owed.
  • A wrong payment: Report a mistake. It is cancelled after approval.
  • A debt recorded by mistake: Cancel the debt (recorded by mistake). After approval the stock goes back to the shop and the sale is marked cancelled.

Who can use it

Those who can sell record payments. Corrections and debt cancellations are approved by the Super Admin.

Good to know

The sale counts in sales on the day it was made. Payments are cash collected, not extra sales.

9Payroll

What it is

Salaries and payments to staff.

How it works

Payroll → New payroll entry: who was paid (a user or a typed name), the month, the amount, the location and a note. It waits for the Chief Admin's approval.

Who can use it

Most roles submit entries (Sales Agents for their own shop). The Chief Admin approves.

Good to know

Salaries are private: only the Chief Admin and Super Admin see every entry. Others see only what they submitted.

10Withdrawals

What it is

Money taken out for no business purpose, e.g. a family expense.

How it works

Withdrawals → New withdrawal: amount, who took it, date, location and reason. The Chief Admin approves it.

Good to know

A withdrawal is never counted as a business cost. It is shown apart from profit, so owners see the real picture.

11Cash

What it is

The cash the business has now: Cash on hand.

How it works

  • Cash on hand = Opening cash + Cash sales + Credit paid in cash − Cash refunds − Withdrawals. Mobile money and unpaid credit are not counted.
  • Add opening cash for money you already had that didn't come from a sale: amount, date, who received it, where it is, and a note.
  • Amounts above the Approval limit (USD) wait for approval. A mistake is undone with Reverse, never deleted.

Who can use it

The Super Admin (owner) only, by default.

Good to know

Cash figures never count in sales, margin or profit.

12Approvals

What it is

One place for every decision before a change takes effect.

How it works

Open Approvals (the menu shows a number when something waits). Open a request, see who asked, why and what changes, then Approve or Reject. Your own requests are under Requested by me.

RequestDecided by (default)
Requisition, stock batch, stock distribution, payroll, withdrawal, deletionChief Admin
Sale edit, sale exchange (refund), credit payment correctionSuper Admin
Credit debt cancelled (mistake), opening cashSuper Admin

Good to know

If the change can't be applied any more (e.g. the stock is gone), the approval is refused and nothing changes.

13Analytics

What it is

The results of the service: sales, margin, costs, profit, products and days without sales.

How it works

  • Pick a period (Day, Week, Month, Custom) and a location (all or one shop).
  • Cards: Net profit (hidden until you tap Show net profit), Total sales, Margin, Expenses, Payroll, and Withdrawals shown apart.
  • ▲/▼ compares with the period before, only when both periods are the same length and at least 3 days long. Green is good news: a cost going down is green.
  • Highlights: short notes, e.g. no sales since a date, the share of the margin taken by costs, the best product's share of revenue (never above 100%).
  • Charts: Sales over time and Costs over time. Lists: Most sold, Least sold, Top profit contributors, Unsold stock. The Products sold report has Download (CSV).

Days with no sales

What it is

A list of the shop days where no sale was recorded, so forgotten sales get added.

How it works

  • A day counts as having no sales when it is a past open day of the shop, nothing was sold, and nobody marked it closed. Counting starts from the shop's first sale. Today is never counted.
  • On the sales chart: a small amber dot for each such day. Closed days are shaded.
  • Under the chart, Days with no sales: Add sales opens the sale form with the day and shop filled in (up to 7 days back; any day for the Super Admin). Or Mark as closed / no sales, with an optional note (e.g. public holiday). Undo brings the day back.
  • Home shows the number of days without sales this month.

Who can use it

Analytics: Chief Admin and Super Admin. Marking a day closed: whoever can record sales at that shop. The Home row: whoever sees the shop's sales.

Good to know

Each service marks its own days: a shop can sell hair but no clothes on the same day. The opening days are set per shop in Locations.

14Reports

What it is

A short summary of a service for a period, saved so it can be opened again. It only reads the figures: it never changes money or stock.

Where to find it

Reports. The bell at the top tells you when a report is ready.

How it works

  • Highlights at the top: 3 or 4 short lines about sales, days with no sales, stock to reorder and money owed.
  • Sales: total sales, Margin, Expenses, Payroll, Net profit and Withdrawals (shown apart), Sales per shop and Top 5 products. They are compared with the period of the same length just before, only when that period has figures. When some shop days have no sales registered, the report warns that the totals may be lower than the real figures.
  • Days with no sales registered: the number of days per shop and the dates, with days in a row shown as one range (open days only; days marked Closed / no sales are left out). For periods longer than 31 days, only the latest 14 dates are listed.
  • Low and sold-out stock: each product with Sold in last 30 days and a Suggested reorder (enough for 30 days of sales, and at least the low stock level, minus what is on hand), plus the Stock value (purchase cost).
  • Waiting for approval: each request with its type, amount, who asked and when. Customers owe: each customer with the oldest unpaid date and the days unpaid, oldest debts first. Cash: cash on hand and opening cash waiting for approval.
  • Each report says Based on data entered by a date and time. Sales entered later are not in it.
  • Generate report: pick any period up to 12 months. The report is made at once and saved.
  • Report settings: switch on Send the report automatically, then choose how often (every day, week or month), the day, the time (Lubumbashi time), what to include and who receives it. By default: every Sunday at 09:00, covering Monday to Sunday of that week.
  • Every saved report opens from the list. Download PDF gives the same report as a PDF.
  • Reports are sent In the app. Email is coming soon.

Who can use it

The Super Admin and the Chief Admin. The Cash part is shown to the owner only. Each person sets up their own scheduled report.

Good to know

If a scheduled report can't be made, it is tried again once. If it still fails, it shows as Failed and you can generate it yourself. Every report is written in the audit log.

15Administration

What it is

Managing people, roles, locations, and checking the history of changes. In the Admin menu at the top.

How it works

  • Manage users: create a user (name, email, role, location). A strong password is shown once, to give to the person. Change role or location, Block / Unblock.
  • Manage roles: turn permissions on or off per role, with own location or all locations.
  • Locations: shops and warehouses, shared by every service. Set Days we open for each shop (Monday to Saturday by default). Retire a location by unticking Active. Locations are never deleted.
  • Audit log: every change, who did it, when, before and after.

Who can use it

Users, roles and locations: Super Admin. Audit log: Chief Admin and Super Admin.

16Rules that protect your data

  • Everything is recorded. Every add, change and delete goes to the audit log, which can't be edited.
  • Nothing is deleted straight away. A delete waits for an approval. Records in use are archived instead.
  • Mistakes are undone with a correction, not by erasing: a reversal, a correction request or an exchange.
  • Stock never goes below zero, and two people can't sell the same last piece.
  • Nobody approves their own request (Super Admin excepted, and marked).
  • Withdrawals and cash are never profit. Profit = margin − expenses − payroll.
