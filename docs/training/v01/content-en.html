1Getting started

The platform manages the whole business: products, stock requests, purchases abroad, stock in Goma and in the shops, sales, payroll, withdrawals and analytics.

Services

The company runs several services. Each one has its own products, stock, sales and figures:

  • Hair — wigs, bundles, closures… told apart by length (inches) and colour.
  • Fashion (Mode in French) — clothes, told apart by size and colour, with a photo.
  • Housing and Transport — Coming soon.

Locations (shops and warehouses) are shared by every service: Boutique la Grace 1 can sell Hair and Fashion. Each service's stock and sales at that shop stay separate.

Signing in

  1. Open the platform and sign in with your email and password. Accounts are created by the Super Admin — there is no self sign-up.
  2. The first time, you must choose a new password (at least 8 characters).
  3. Choose a service on the Choose a service screen. The one you used last is marked Last used.
Blocked accounts. If the Super Admin blocks an account, that person is signed out and cannot sign in again until unblocked.

The screen

  • Side menu (on a phone: the menu button): Home, Analytics, Products, Requisitions, Stock, Sales, Expenses, Payroll, Withdrawals, Approvals, and at the bottom Product settings and Switch service. You only see the modules your role allows.
  • Top bar: Switch service, administration links (for administrators), the language selector, Log out.
  • The Approvals item shows a number when requests are waiting for you.
Phones first. Everything works on a phone: long tables turn into cards, and buttons are large enough to tap.
Version 01 notes. The Expenses module (rent, electricity…) is not available yet — today only purchase-trip expenses are recorded, inside purchase batches. Correcting or refunding a sale will come in a later version. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).

"Own shop" and "anywhere"

Each permission has a reach. Own location means the person can only act at the location set on their account (a Sales Agent sells only in their shop — the platform fills it in and refuses any other). All locations means anywhere.

Golden rule: nobody approves their own request. The person who asks and the person who approves must be different. The only exception is the Super Admin, whose self-approvals are allowed and clearly marked in the audit log.

The Super Admin can do everything. Their own role can't be edited, and the last active Super Admin can't be blocked or demoted.

4Products and prices

The catalogue

Open Products → Catalogue. Each product gets an automatic code (SKU): HAIR-00001 for Hair, FASH-00001 for Fashion.

ServiceCategoriesHow products are told apart
HairWigs, Bundles, Closures, Frontals, Extensions, Hair care, AccessoriesLength (inches), colour, texture
FashionDresses, Tops & shirts, Trousers & jeans, Skirts, Suits & sets, Jackets & coats, Shoes, Bags, AccessoriesSize, colour, and one photo
  1. Click New product, fill in the name, category and unit (piece, pair, set…), then the length or size and the colour from the lists.
  2. Fashion: add a photo (an image up to 5 MB — on a phone you can take it with the camera). It then appears in the sale form and the product pickers.
  3. Save. Search works on the name, SKU, length, size and colour.
Pending confirmation. A product created by someone who can't confirm products starts as pending; a Chief Admin or Chief Sales Admin confirms it (never their own, except the Super Admin).
Archive, don't delete. A product used in a requisition or stock can only be archived. Deleting an unused product needs an approval.

Product settings

Product settings holds the lists used by product forms: lengths (Hair), sizes (Fashion — use the arrows to set the order, e.g. XS, S, M, L, XL) and colours. Values in use can be deactivated but not deleted.

Prices

Products → Prices shows, for each product, its selling price next to what it was bought at (every lot), what's on hand and the last sale. Open a product to set its selling price. This price fills the sale form automatically; sellers can still enter another price, with a reason.

5Requisitions

A requisition is a shop's request for stock. Once approved, it is ready to be purchased.

{{diagram:requisition}}
  1. Requisitions → New requisition: choose the location and add a note.
  2. Add product for each item, with the quantity — or leave it empty and describe the need in the note (for example "New stock for the shop, dresses and shoes, about $500"). The buyer will then choose the products.
  3. Submit for approval. The requisition is locked while it waits.
  4. The Chief Admin approves or rejects it in Approvals. If rejected, revise it and submit again.

Approved requisitions appear under the Ready to purchase filter. When the buyer adds them to a purchase, the status becomes Purchasing, then Closed once everything is resolved. Products the buyer adds to a requisition are marked Added by buyer.

6Purchasing abroad (purchase batches)

A purchase batch is one buying trip: what was bought, what wasn't, what it cost and the trip's expenses. It's done by the Chief Inventory Admin (the buyer).

{{diagram:batch}}

Building the batch

  1. Stock → Purchase batches → New batch: give it a title (e.g. "Guangzhou, October").
  2. Add from requisitions: tick the approved lines to buy. An empty requisition shows its note and an Add products button.
  3. Add extra product: for anything else. Choose For requisition to attach it to an approved requisition, or None (extra purchase). Use New product to create a product on the spot.
  4. For each line: Purchased with quantity and unit cost, or Not purchased with a reason. Buying fewer than requested also needs a reason.
  5. Add expense for the trip: category (transport, freight, customs, meals…), amount, note and a receipt (photo or PDF up to 10 MB).
Expenses stay separate. Trip expenses never change the product costs. They can be added at any time — even after the batch has arrived — by the buyer and by the Goma team (for example transport paid on arrival).

After buying

  1. Mark as purchased: the lines and costs are locked, and the requisition lines are marked purchased, partial or not purchased.
  2. Submit for approval: the Chief Admin approves the batch. If something must change before approval, use Request reopen.
  3. Mark shipped when the goods leave. The Goma team then uses Mark arrived.

7Receiving in Goma

When a batch arrives, the Manager Admin or Chief Sales Admin counts what actually arrived. This is the moment the goods become sellable stock.

  1. Open the batch (status Arrived) and scroll to Receiving in Goma.
  2. For each purchased line enter Received (good pieces) and Damaged. Missing is calculated for you.
  3. If anything is damaged or missing, give a reason.
  4. Confirm receipt. The batch becomes Received.
What happens. Only the good pieces become stock. They are held by the business (not yet in a shop), each line as a lot at its purchase cost. The count can't be changed after confirming.

8Distribution to shops and people

A distribution sends stock from the business to a shop or a person. The Chief Admin approves it.

{{diagram:holders}}
  1. Stock → Distributions → New distribution.
  2. Choose A location or A person.
  3. Enter the quantity for each lot. You can't ask for more than is available (on hand minus other pending distributions).
  4. Submit for approval.
{{diagram:distribution}}
Safe by design. When the Chief Admin approves, the platform checks the stock again. If it's no longer there, the approval is refused and nothing moves.

9Stock overview and report

Stock → Overview shows the units on hand, the stock value (purchase cost) and how many products are low or out of stock.

By product — the stock report

Every product of the service, including finished ones, with: status, on hand, sold, received and last sold. It opens on In stock.

StatusMeaning
In stockPieces are available.
Low stockAt or below the product's "low stock" level.
Out of stockNone left anywhere, with the date it ran out ("since …").
Never stockedCreated, but never received.
  • Use the status chips and the search to find products quickly.
  • Download (CSV) exports the list for Excel.
  • Open a product to see where it is now (each shop or person) and its history: received (BATCH-…), sent (DIST-…), sold (SALE-…), with the running total.
  • Price managers can set Low stock at (e.g. 5) for each product.

By lot and By holder

By lot shows each purchase lot (batch, cost, received, on hand). By holder shows what each shop, person or the business holds.

Stock never goes negative. Every receipt, distribution and sale goes through the same check; the platform refuses anything that would take more than is there.

10Sales

Sales are recorded on the phone, in the shop, as they happen.

  1. Sales → New sale.
  2. Sale date: today by default. Forgot a sale? Pick the day it happened — up to 7 days back.
  3. Location: Sales Agents are locked to their shop; chiefs choose.
  4. Add a product: search by name, SKU, length, size or colour (photos in Fashion).
  5. Choose the lot (batch · cost · date · pieces left), the quantity and the price. The suggested price is filled in; a different price needs a short reason.
  6. Payment: Cash, Mobile money or Credit (credit needs the customer's name).
  7. Record sale. If something is missing, the bottom bar says what.
{{diagram:phone}}
No overselling. You can't sell more than the shop holds. If two people try to sell the last piece at the same moment, only one sale goes through.

The sales list

Sales lists every sale with its number (SALE-00001…), date, location, seller, payment, total and margin. Filter by dates and location; open a sale to see its lines and any price reason. Sales recorded on a later day show a Recorded … badge. Sales Agents see only their own shop's sales.

11Payroll

  1. Payroll → New payroll entry.
  2. Paid to: a person on the platform, or someone else by name.
  3. The month, the amount (USD), the location and a note (e.g. "first half of the month").
  4. Submit for approval. The Chief Admin approves it — never their own entry.
Salaries are private. Only the Chief Admin and the Super Admin see every entry. Everyone else sees only the entries they submitted.

The page shows the approved and pending totals, with filters by month, location and status.

12Withdrawals

A withdrawal is money taken that serves no business purpose (for example a family expense).

  1. Withdrawals → New withdrawal.
  2. The amount, who took it (you by default), the date (today or earlier), the location (the till) and a reason.
  3. Submit for approval. Only the Chief Admin (or the Super Admin) approves withdrawals.
Not a business cost. Withdrawals are never subtracted from profit. They are shown separately so the owners see the real picture.

14Analytics

Analytics (Chief Admin and Super Admin) shows the service's results.

  • Filters: Day, Week (from Monday), Month, Custom (up to 366 days), and Location (all or one shop). Dates are in Lubumbashi time.
  • Cards: total sales, margin, expenses, payroll, net profit, and withdrawals shown apart.
  • Lists: most sold, least sold and best margin products.
  • Charts: sales (with margin), expenses and payroll over time.

How the figures are calculated

{{diagram:money}}
  • Margin = sale price − purchase cost of the pieces sold.
  • Net profit = margin − expenses − payroll. In red when negative.
  • Expenses in version 01 are the purchase-trip expenses, counted on the day they were recorded. Payroll counts on the day it was approved.
Example above: a whole trip's expenses ($518.60) were recorded this month while only a few pieces were sold yet, so net profit is negative. It improves as the rest of the stock sells.

15Administration

Manage users (Super Admin)

  1. Manage users → Create user: name, email, role and — for roles limited to their own shop — a location.
  2. A strong password is shown once. Copy it and give it to the person; they must change it at first sign-in.
  3. Change a person's role or location at any time; Block / Unblock to stop or allow access.

Manage roles (Super Admin)

Turn permissions on or off for a role, and choose Own location or All locations for each. Changes apply immediately. The Super Admin role can't be edited.

Locations (Super Admin)

Shops and warehouses, shared by every service. Locations are never deleted — untick Active to retire one.

Audit log (Chief Admin, Super Admin)

Audit log lists every change: who did what, when, with the values before and after. It can't be edited or deleted.

16Quick reference by role

Sales Agent

  • Record each sale: Sales → New sale.
  • Forgot one? Pick the sale date (up to 7 days back).
  • Check your shop's sales in Sales.
  • Request withdrawals and submit payroll for your shop.

Manager Admin

  • Mark arrived, then count in Receiving in Goma and Confirm receipt.
  • Send stock: Stock → Distributions → New distribution.
  • Add expenses paid on arrival to the batch.
  • Watch Stock → Overview.

Chief Sales Admin

  • Create requisitions for the shops.
  • Receive and distribute stock like the Manager.
  • Sell at any location; set selling prices in Prices.
  • Confirm pending products.

Chief Inventory Admin (buyer)

  • Purchase batches → New batch; add requisition lines and extra products.
  • Set unit costs, add trip expenses with receipts.
  • Mark as purchased → Submit for approval → Mark shipped.
  • Keep Product settings up to date.

Chief Admin

  • Check Approvals every day: requisitions, batches, distributions, payroll, withdrawals, deletions.
  • Follow results in Analytics.
  • Set selling prices; review the audit log.

Super Admin

  • Create users, set roles and locations, block access.
  • Adjust role permissions; manage locations.
  • Can do everything; self-approvals are marked.

17Questions and troubleshooting

"Record sale" doesn't go through.
Read the message in the bottom bar: a price is missing, a reason is needed for a changed price, a credit sale needs the customer's name, or a quantity is more than what's left.
I can't see the "Receiving in Goma" section.
The batch must be Arrived, and your role must be allowed to receive stock (Manager Admin, Chief Sales Admin, Super Admin).
A product disappeared from the stock.
It's not gone: in Stock → By product, choose Out of stock or All.
Why is net profit negative?
Expenses and payroll for the period are bigger than the margin — often because a trip's expenses were recorded before most of its stock was sold.
I can't approve my own request.
That's the rule — another approver must decide.
I forgot to record yesterday's sale.
In New sale, set the Sale date to yesterday (up to 7 days back).
The price field asks for a reason.
You entered a price different from the suggested selling price — type why (offer, loyal customer…).
I can't choose another shop when selling.
Sales Agents sell only at their own location. A chief can sell anywhere.

18Glossary

Service
A business line: Hair, Fashion (Mode)…
Location
A shop or warehouse, shared by every service.
SKU
A product's automatic code, e.g. HAIR-00003.
Requisition (REQ-…)
A shop's request for stock.
Purchase batch (BATCH-…)
One buying trip: lines bought, costs and trip expenses.
Lot
The pieces of one product received from one batch, at one purchase cost.
Holder
Who holds stock: the business, a location or a person.
Distribution (DIST-…)
Stock sent from the business to a shop or person.
Sale (SALE-…)
A recorded sale; it takes stock out of a shop.
Suggested price
The selling price set in Prices; filled in automatically on sales.
Margin
Sale price minus the purchase cost of the pieces sold.
Net profit
Margin minus expenses minus payroll.
Withdrawal
Money taken for no business purpose; shown apart from profit.
Approval
A decision by the Chief Admin before a change takes effect.
Audit log
The permanent record of every change.
